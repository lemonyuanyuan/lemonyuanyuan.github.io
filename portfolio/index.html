    Biography

    I am Xinyuan Wang (王心元), an undergraduate in Robotics at the University of Michigan. I have worked with Prof. Jiaoyang Li at Carnegie Mellon University, Dr. Tribhi Kathuria at the University of Michigan, and Prof. Zhongqiang Ren at Shanghai Jiao Tong University.

    I’m interested in algorithmic robotics, particularly motion planning for multi-robot and human-interactive environments. I leverage tools in optimization, search, and learning to help robots coordinate efficiently and reliably with one another and with people.

    Selected Projects

    Hierarchical Policy Learning for Socially Aware Robot Navigation
    Xinyuan Wang, Tribhi Kathuria
    In submission to ICRA, 2027

    Introducing a hierarchical policy learning framework that combines interaction-level reasoning and motion control to enable socially aware navigation in narrow human–robot encounters.

    Mixed-Integer Motion Planning for Multiple Traveling Salesmen Problems with Connected Visitation
    Xinyuan Wang, Lekang Zhou, Runhui Long, Zhitong Tang, Zhongqiang Ren
    In submission to T-RO, 2026

    Formulating multi-robot task allocation and motion planning under connectivity constraints as a mixed-integer optimization problem with efficient convex reformulations.

    Model-Based Geometry-Aware Generative Optimization for Constrained Locomotion
    Zhilin He, Xinyuan Wang, Changliu Liu
    In submission to RA-L, 2026

    Developing a geometry-aware optimization framework for constrained humanoid locomotion, with real-world validation on the Unitree G1 in narrow and obstacle-rich environments.

    Walking on Rough Terrain with Any Number of Legs
    Zhuoyang Chen, Xinyuan Wang, Shai Revzen
    Bioinspiration & Biomimetics, 2026  /  paper

    Investigating bio-inspired legged locomotion through a systematic study of control strategies and simulation-based evaluation on rough terrain.
